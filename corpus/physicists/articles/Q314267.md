# Jagadish Chandra Bose

Sir Jagadish Chandra Bose 
(;, IPA: [dʒɔɡodiʃ tʃɔndro boʃu]; 30 November 1858 – 23 November 1937) was a biologist, physicist, botanist. He was also a writer of science fiction.
